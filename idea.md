Here is the comprehensive product design specification for the new Referral Management Platform. This document is tailored for product stakeholders, UX/UI designers, and frontend teams to guide the creation of wireframes, prototypes, and the final user experience.

---

# 1. Product Vision

Our vision is to transform the referral management process from a manual, spreadsheet-driven administrative burden into a seamless, **closed-loop referral ecosystem**. By creating a frictionless SaaS experience, we will eliminate patient leakage, reduce time-to-consultation, and foster secure, high-quality collaboration between our internal primary care providers and external specialists. The platform will deliver actionable insights to administrators, zero-barrier access to external specialists, and empowering transparency to patients.

# 2. User Personas

### Persona 1: Internal Staff / Referral Coordinator (Endpoint: `/admin`)
*   **User Goals:** Ensure every patient is matched with the right specialist, track referral statuses across 10+ facilities, prevent referrals from falling through the cracks, and reduce administrative overhead.
*   **Typical Daily Workflow:** Logs in at 8:00 AM. Reviews the "Referral Aging" dashboard to find patients who haven't been scheduled. Follows up with external clinics, processes new outbound referrals generated by internal providers, handles bounced referrals (e.g., insurance denied), and runs weekly reports on specialist responsiveness.

### Persona 2: External Specialist (Endpoint: `/specialist`)
*   **User Goals:** Receive vetted, accurate referrals with patient context, quickly triage and schedule patients, and communicate outcomes back to the referring provider without needing yet another heavy EHR login. 
*   **Typical Daily Workflow:** Dr. Carl (Cardiologist) or his front-desk manager receives an SMS/Email about a new referral. They click a secure link, enter an OTP, view patient demographics, accept the referral, and trigger a scheduling call. After the visit, they upload a brief summary to close the loop.

### Persona 3: Patient (Endpoint: `/patient`)
*   **User Goals:** Know exactly who they are being referred to, understand the next steps, and feel supported throughout their care journey.
*   **Typical Daily Workflow:** Receives an SMS that a referral to Cardiology has been created. Clicks a secure link to view Dr. Carl’s clinic details, address, and instructions on what to expect.

# 3. Referral Lifecycle

To build a closed-loop system, UX/UI must design distinct visual indicators (badges, color coding) for each status.

**The Happy Path:**
`Referral Created` (Internal Staff) → `Sent to Specialist` (System) → `Viewed` (Specialist) → `Accepted` (Specialist) → `Appointment Scheduled` (Specialist or Admin) → `Patient Seen` (Specialist) → `Consultation Completed/Notes Shared` (Specialist) → `Closed` (System/Admin).

**Alternative Paths & Exceptions (Requires UI handling):**
*   **Denied:** Specialist reviews and declines (e.g., lack of specific sub-specialty). *Actionable by: Specialist.*
*   **Insurance Not Accepted:** Specialist office verifies insurance and rejects. *Actionable by: Specialist.*
*   **Unable To Contact Patient:** Specialist attempted outreach 3 times. Returns referral to internal team. *Actionable by: Specialist.*
*   **Provider Out Of Network (OON):** Flagged during creation or by specialist. *Actionable by: Admin/Specialist.*
*   **Referral Expired:** No action taken within 30 days. *Actionable by: System (auto-updates).*
*   **Referral Reassigned:** Admin pulls a referral from an unresponsive specialist and sends it to a new one. *Actionable by: Admin.*

# 4. Patient Experience & Journey

We must ensure patients are engaged without creating technical barriers. 

### Exploration of Approaches:
*   **Option A: Status Updates Only (One-way SMS/Email).** *Pros:* Zero friction. *Cons:* Patients cannot self-serve, confirm appointments, or get directions. It feels outdated.
*   **Option B: Patient Self-Service Portal (Account Creation).** *Pros:* Full feature set. *Cons:* High friction. Patients will not create and remember a username/password for a one-off specialist referral. High drop-off rate.
*   **Option C: Magic-Link Patient Experience.** *Pros:* Low friction, highly secure. Patient receives an SMS with a unique URL. Clicking it prompts for a DOB verification to unlock a beautiful, read-only tracking page. 

**Recommendation:** **Option C (Magic-Link).** It provides the perfect balance of security, modern UX, and zero-barrier access.

### Patient Journey Walkthrough:
1. Patient's primary provider creates a referral.
2. Patient receives an SMS: *"Your referral to Cardiology is ready. Tap here to view details."*
3. Patient taps the link, enters their Date of Birth to authenticate.
4. Patient sees a visual timeline (Created → Accepted → Scheduled).
5. Patient views Dr. Carl’s clinic address (with an Apple/Google Maps button) and phone number.

# 5. Specialist Journey (Scenario Walkthrough)

**Scenario:** Patient requires a cardiac consultation. Internal provider creates a referral for Dr. Carl.

1. **Specialist Engagement:** Dr. Carl receives an email and SMS: *"New Urgent Referral from [Our Organization] for Patient ID: 8932. Click to view."*
2. **Authentication:** Dr. Carl clicks the link. The screen prompts: *"Send OTP to ending in -4556"*. Dr. Carl enters the 6-digit OTP. 
3. **Review:** Dr. Carl views the referral details (MRN, phone, specialty, referring provider NPI). 
4. **Action:** Dr. Carl clicks the **"Accept Referral"** CTA button.
5. **Patient Communication:** Upon acceptance, the system auto-texts the patient: *"Dr. Carl has accepted your referral. His office will call you within 48 hours."*
6. **Appointment:** Dr. Carl's staff schedules the appointment and updates the status in the portal to `Appointment Scheduled`.
7. **Referral Completion:** Post-visit, Dr. Carl accesses the portal via the same secure link, changes status to `Consultation Completed`, and uses a drag-and-drop UI to upload a PDF consultation summary.

---

# 6. Admin Journey

Internal referral coordinators live in this platform. Their journey is focused on exception management and throughput.

1. **Morning Triage:** Admin logs into the Dashboard. They look at the "Referral Aging" widget to see referrals stuck in `Sent to Specialist` for > 72 hours.
2. **Bulk Action:** They select 10 pending referrals and click **"Send Reminder to Specialists"**.
3. **Creation:** A new spreadsheet request comes in (or is verbalized). Admin clicks a persistent "+ New Referral" button. They input the required fields (Patient MRN, Phone, Email, Target Specialty, etc.).
4. **Intervention:** Admin notices Dr. Carl denied a referral due to "Insurance Not Accepted." Admin uses the global search, finds the patient, clicks "Reassign," and sends the referral to Dr. Smith instead.
5. **Reporting:** At month-end, Admin navigates to the Reports tab to download a CSV of all closed referrals for leadership.

# 7. Screen Inventory

### A. Internal Admin App (`/admin`)

**Screen: Global Dashboard**
*   **Purpose:** High-level operational overview.
*   **Key Data:** Widgets for Total Active Referrals, Bottlenecks (aging > 7 days), Top 5 Specialists by Volume.
*   **Actions:** Quick links to action-needed queues.

**Screen: Referral Queue (The core workspace)**
*   **Purpose:** Manage and update all referrals.
*   **Key Data:** Table containing Patient Name/MRN, Date, Specialty, Assigned To, Status, Last Updated.
*   **Available Actions:** Reassign, Cancel, Edit, Bulk Remind.
*   **Filters/Search:** Filter by Status, Facility, Specialty. Search by Patient Name or MRN.

**Screen: Referral Detail View (Slide-out drawer or dedicated page)**
*   **Purpose:** Deep dive into a single referral.
*   **Key Data:** Full patient demographics, referring provider details, full audit timeline (Who did what, when), attached documents.
*   **Primary Buttons:** Edit Status, Add Note, Reassign.

### B. Specialist App (`/specialist`)

**Screen: Secure Login (OTP)**
*   **Purpose:** Frictionless access without passwords.
*   **Actions:** Request OTP, Submit OTP.

**Screen: Specialist Workspace**
*   **Purpose:** Single-page view of referrals assigned to this specific provider.
*   **Key Data:** Patient Name/Phone, Reason for Referral, Status.
*   **Primary Buttons:** Accept, Reject, Request Info.
*   **Secondary Actions:** Schedule Appointment (opens modal to enter date/time), Mark Seen, Upload Notes.

### C. Patient App (`/patient`)

**Screen: Magic Link Authentication**
*   **Purpose:** Secure entry.
*   **Actions:** Enter DOB, Submit.

**Screen: Patient Tracking Hub**
*   **Purpose:** Inform and empower the patient.
*   **Key Data:** Visual progress bar (Created, Accepted, Scheduled). Specialist Name, Clinic Address, Phone Number, What to Bring (ID, Insurance).
*   **Primary Buttons:** Get Directions (Maps integration), Call Clinic.

# 8. Navigation Structure

**Internal Staff (`/admin`) Sidebar Navigation:**
*   `Dashboard` (Home icon)
*   `Referrals` (List icon - the main queue)
*   `Providers` (Stethoscope icon - directory of external specialists)
*   `Patients` (User icon - patient directory and history)
*   `Reports` (Chart icon)
*   `Settings` (Gear icon - manage facilities, users)

**Specialist (`/specialist`) Top Navigation (Simplified):**
*   `My Referrals` (Active queue)
*   `Completed` (Historical referrals)
*   `Clinic Profile` (Update contact info)

**Patient (`/patient`) - No Navigation Menu:**
*   Single Page Application (SPA) scrolling view focused entirely on the specific referral context to avoid confusion.

# 9. Notifications Strategy

*   **Internal Staff:** Daily morning digest email summary. In-app bell icon for exceptions (e.g., Specialist rejected a referral).
*   **Specialist:** 
    *   *Trigger: New Referral.* Medium: Email + SMS.
    *   *Trigger: Overdue Action (48 hrs).* Medium: Email.
    *   *Trigger: Message from Admin.* Medium: Email with secure link.
*   **Patient:** 
    *   *Trigger: Referral Created.* Medium: SMS.
    *   *Trigger: Specialist Accepted (Match made).* Medium: SMS.
    *   *Trigger: Appointment Scheduled.* Medium: SMS.

# 10. Reporting & Analytics

The `/admin` Dashboard must include robust operational reporting:
*   **Provider Performance Matrix:** Time-to-view, time-to-accept, and time-to-schedule metrics grouped by specialist. Helps administrators identify which external specialists provide the best patient experience.
*   **Referral Aging:** A histogram showing referrals grouped by days untouched (0-3, 4-7, 8-14, 15+).
*   **Leakage/Denial Report:** Breakdown of *why* referrals are not completed (Insurance, Unreachable, Out of Network).
*   **Volume by Specialty:** A pie or bar chart showing the highest demanded specialties to inform future internal hiring strategies.

# 11. Security Considerations

To ensure compliance without dictating implementation:
*   **HIPAA Compliance:** All Patient Health Information (PHI) is encrypted at rest and in transit. The patient magic-link must *not* contain PHI in the URL itself (use a generic hash) and must require secondary verification (DOB) before displaying data.
*   **Audit Logging:** Every status change, document upload, and view must be logged securely (Timestamp, User ID, IP address, Action Taken). Visible to Admins in the Referral Detail View.
*   **OTP Authentication:** Specialists use their NPI-registered phone number or email to receive short-lived (e.g., 5 minute) 6-digit codes.
*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Internal staff can see all facilities. External specialists can *only* see patients explicitly referred to their specific clinic.

# 12. Recommended MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

To replace the spreadsheets immediately and deliver rapid value:
1.  **Admin UI:** Manual referral creation form, basic grid queue with status filters, and ability to manually update statuses.
2.  **Specialist Experience:** Secure OTP magic links, ability to view referral, and update status (Accept/Reject/Complete).
3.  **Patient Experience:** Automated one-way SMS notifications when a referral is created and accepted (Option A for MVP to save time, bridging to Option C in Phase 2).
4.  **Core statuses:** Created, Sent, Accepted, Rejected, Completed.

---

# 13. Recommended Phase 2 Enhancements

Once the MVP proves successful and user adoption stabilizes, we should design for:
1.  **Patient Magic-Link Portal (Option C):** Full rollout of the visual tracking timeline.
2.  **EHR Integration (HL7/FHIR):** Automate referral creation directly from the internal provider's Electronic Health Record (EHR) rather than manual admin data entry. 
3.  **Smart Specialist Matching:** AI-driven recommendations for admins based on specialist zip code, insurance accepted, and historical response times.
4.  **Patient Self-Scheduling:** Allowing the patient to pick an appointment slot from the external specialist’s calendar directly via their mobile link.
5.  **Secure Bi-Directional Messaging:** An in-app chat thread tied to the referral record, allowing the Admin and Specialist to securely text questions (e.g., "Need latest lab results") without picking up the phone.
