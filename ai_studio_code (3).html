The Platform Ecosystem

Three distinct personas. One unified goal. Discover how our targeted interfaces solve specific bottlenecks in the referral lifecycle.

Frictionless Auth

Specialists suffer from portal fatigue. We use contextual magic links and OTP so providers can securely access patient data without creating passwords.

Smart Triage Engine

Instead of browsing analytics, internal admins get an "Inbox Zero" action hub. It surfaces only stalled or denied referrals that require human intervention.

Patient-Driven Care

A mobile-first companion empowers patients to update their own appointment statuses and upload consult documents using their smartphone camera.
